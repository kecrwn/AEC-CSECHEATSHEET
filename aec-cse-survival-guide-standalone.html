FIRST ACTION

Fast route: official links & portals

AEC identifies itself as established in 1998, AICTE-approved, MAKAUT-affiliated and UGC 2(f)-recognised. Use AEC for college-specific information, MAKAUT Exam for university examination notices, and the current MAKAUT NEP index for curriculum. [01] [02] [03]

!

Do not use a random ERP link forwarded in a chat group. AEC shows Student Login in public navigation, but no stable official ERP URL was verified. Ask during induction or use the AEC central route. Never share passwords, OTPs, registration numbers or fee-payment credentials through a third-party form.

KEY CSE ENTRY COURSE · 26-ES-CS201

Programming for Problem Solving and Introduction to AI

Assumes no prior programming. The official map moves from computer basics and algorithms through C, functions, pointers, files, search/sort and efficiency—then closes with AI concepts, agents and applications.

ASSESSMENTCA 30 + ESE 70

At least half of Groups B and C involve writing or reading C code; one Group C question covers AI. [10]
