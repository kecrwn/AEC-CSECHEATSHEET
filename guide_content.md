## 2. First-year CSE curriculum: current official MAKAUT release

MAKAUT’s current NEP index lists **B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering, Course Code 001**, effective from **AY 2026–27**, and was last updated on 21 August 2026. It links one common curriculum structure and detailed first-year Semester I and Semester II PDFs. The PDFs explicitly say first-year is common across B.Tech programmes, with a Group A rotation for CSE and allied branches. [6] [9] [10]

### Course-map: Programming for Problem Solving and Introduction to AI

This is the key CSE entry course in Semester II. It assumes no prior programming and introduces computational thinking and C from first principles through pointers, structures and files, alongside an AI introduction. Its eight modules cover: computer basics; computer generations; problem solving, algorithms and flowcharts; C fundamentals; functions and recursion; arrays, strings and pointers; structures, files, search, sort and basic algorithm efficiency; and an introduction to AI, including weak/strong AI, machine/deep learning in outline, agents and applications. The paper is **CA 30 + ESE 70** and the official note says at least half of Groups B and C involve writing or reading C code, with at least one Group C question on AI. [10]

Track the index in this guide and add later-semester documents only after MAKAUT publishes them. [6]

## 3. Calendar, assessment and examination checkpoints

MAKAUT’s official **Provisional Academic Calendar 2026–27** is exactly that: provisional. It sets 30 July 2026 as commencement for all odd-semester programmes; first-semester commencement and enrolment appear as “after registration.” [14]

| Period | Odd semester 2026–27 | Even semester 2026–27 |
|---|---|---|
| Academic commencement | 30 July 2026 (all odd semesters); First Semester “after registration” | 4 January 2027 |
| CA cycle | CA1: 7–12 Sep; CA2: 2–7 Nov; CA3/online MCQ: 17–21 Nov | CA1: 15–19 Feb; CA2: 22–26 Mar; CA3: 29 Mar–2 Apr; CA4: 5–9 Apr; end-semester CA / online MCQ: 12–16 Apr |
| Attendance / WCA | WCA: 17–21 Nov; attendance upload: 18–20 Nov | WCA: 12–16 Apr; attendance upload: 13–15 Apr |
| Exam form fill-up | 23 Nov–2 Dec 2026 | 19–27 Apr 2027 |
| Theory examinations | 3–19 Dec 2026 | 30 Apr–15 May 2027 |
| Practical, sessional, viva | 21–26 Dec 2026 | 17–20 May 2027 |

**Pressure-proof routine:** Save every CA date as soon as AEC communicates its internal schedule; retain fee receipts and acknowledgement numbers; monitor attendance before the published eligibility-upload window; read both the university routine and college circular; and capture a screenshot or PDF of the notice that changes your deadline. For exam action, the **latest official notice overrides this static guide**. [3] [14]
