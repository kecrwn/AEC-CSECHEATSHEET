# AECCHEATSHEET Design Direction

## Chosen approach: Curriculum Terminal

**Design movement.** An editorial technical field manual translated into a digital academic route map.

**Core principles.** Information behaves like a record rather than marketing copy; structure visibly guides a first-year reader; uncertainty is labelled rather than concealed; every high-priority action has a clear source route.

**Color philosophy.** Graphite and dull navy carry the document weight, chalk white handles reading, muted cyan identifies metadata, and Route Yellow is reserved for active routes, critical numbers, verified/high-priority records, and warnings.

**Layout paradigm.** A persistent left folio rail turns the site into a navigational instrument on desktop. Page covers, route maps, source-status strips, thin datum rules and evidence panels translate that system through tablet, mobile and print.

**Signature elements.** A slanted AEC//CSE route-marker glyph, oversized route numerals, and source-map/evidence bands with coordinate-like labels.

**Interaction philosophy.** Navigation and search feel immediate, quiet and operational. Route changes use short fades only; keyboard command search is available without visual clutter.

**Typography system.** Space Grotesk carries route and mission hierarchy; Source Serif supports explanatory text; IBM Plex Mono is limited to codes, source status and operational metadata.

**Brand essence.** AECCHEATSHEET is a verified, first-year B.Tech CSE route map for AEC students who need useful information before the deadline arrives. Personality: precise, calm, protective.

**Brand voice.** Direct, cautionary and source-aware. Example: “One verified route beats ten forwarded screenshots.” Example: “Quote the reporting basis. Recheck the current report.”

## Style Decisions

- The field-manual framing is the core identity: route numbers, source-status labels, thin datum rules, evidence bands and verified-record language appear in every major module.
- Route Yellow functions only as an operational signal: active route, primary action, verified/high-priority record, warning or critical numeral.
- Dense reference pages open with a visual summary strip before tables or rows, and place source class alongside the information it qualifies.
- The AEC//CSE lockup, route-marker glyph and source-map motif recur in sidebar, covers, dividers and evidence bands.
