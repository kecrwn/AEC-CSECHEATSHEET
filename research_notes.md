### MAKAUT first-year structure, extracted from the official Semester I PDF

For **CSE and allied branches**, the official PDF identifies Semester I as a **20-credit** semester. It contains Mathematics I (26-BS-M101; 3 credits); **Physics** (26-BS-PH101; 3 credits) and Physics Lab (26-BS-PH191; 1.5 credits); Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering (26-ES-EE101; 4 credits) and lab (26-ES-EE191; 1.5 credits); English for Communication Skills (26-AEC-101; 2 credits); Engineering Graphics and Design (26-ES-ME191; 2 credits); NCC/NSS/Yoga and Sports (26-VAC-181; 2 credits); Skill Enhancement Course — MOOCs/Skills (26-SEC-181; 1 credit); and Indian Constitution (26-MC-181; non-credit). A three-week, mandatory non-credit induction with Universal Human Values precedes the semester. [Official PDF: sem126.pdf]

The official rotation table says CSE and allied branches take **Physics, Physics Lab, Engineering Graphics, and Indian Constitution** in Semester I; **Chemistry, Chemistry Lab, Workshop Practice, and Indian Knowledge System** rotate into Semester II. The current MAKAUT index does not publish separate detailed curriculum documents for Semesters III–VIII for CSE, so full branch-year course details should be marked as not yet publicly published under the current index rather than inferred from former curricula. [Official PDF: sem126.pdf; https://makautexam.net/aicte_details/aicteugdetailsnep.html]

For CSE and allied branches, the official Semester II PDF specifies **24 credits**, or **28 credits** only for a student exiting with the optional UG certificate who also secures four work-based vocational / summer internship / apprenticeship credits. The regular Semester II table lists Mathematics II (26-BS-M201; 3 credits), Biology for Engineers (26-BS-BI201; 2), **Chemistry** (26-BS-CH201; 3) and Chemistry Lab (26-BS-CH291; 1.5), Programming for Problem Solving and Introduction to AI (26-ES-CS201; 3) and lab / AI Tools (26-ES-CS291; 2), Environmental Science (26-VAC-201; 3), **Workshop Practice** (26-ES-ME292; 2), Language Laboratory (26-AEC-281; 1), Essentials for Professional Skills (26-AEC-282; 1.5), Skill Enhancement Course — MOOCs / Skills (26-SEC-282; 2), and Indian Knowledge System (26-MC-282; non-credit). [Official PDF: sem226.pdf]

The official companion PDF for **Semester II** is headed “Detailed Syllabus — First Year — Semesters II”, indicates the same AY 2026–27 NEP structure, and contains 46 pages. Its downloadable official URL is https://makautexam.net/aicte_details/Syllabusnep/sem226.pdf. Course-table extraction is pending.
